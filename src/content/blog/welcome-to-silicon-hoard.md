Technology leadership at a small or mid-sized organization is a different job than the one most industry writing assumes. The playbooks are built for companies with dedicated architecture teams, procurement departments, and leverage at the negotiating table.

Silicon Hoard is where I write about what that work actually looks like—from the data center floor to the vendor meeting room. Expect practical thinking on infrastructure, strategy, and the decisions that aren't really technical decisions at all.
